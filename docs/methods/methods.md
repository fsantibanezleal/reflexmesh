# Methods

The [numbered method index](README.md) links twelve implemented policies. Each card states its decision rule, training and inference artifacts, emitted evidence, baseline role and empirical limits. [Scientific methods](scientific-methods.md) gives the shared feature, calibration, outcome and evaluation conventions.

M01 and M02 are explicit control baselines. M03 through M08 fit different statistical, recurrent, contextual-bandit, reinforcement-learning and transition models. M09 and M10 measure actual configured language-model planning. M11 fits a paired-outcome deferral gate. M12 combines recurrent candidate scoring, predicted effects, observed residuals and bounded asynchronous deliberation; four component ablations test the contribution of that composition.

These methods share the native authority boundary. A safer executor must not be credited as learned policy superiority. A confidence number names its calibration target; candidate agreement, expected return, computation benefit and safety are different quantities.
